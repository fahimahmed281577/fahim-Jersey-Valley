# Jersey Valley — Product Asset Image Generation Prompts (Google Imagen 3)

This document provides standardized prompt architectures and production prompts specifically optimized for Google Image Generation Models (Imagen 3 / Google Image flow). These prompts are engineered to replace flat vector illustrations with high-end, commercial-grade, photorealistic football jersey product photography.

---

## 1. Google Imagen 3 Prompt Engineering Standards for E-Commerce Apparel

### Core Prompt Formula
```text
[Subject & Framing] + [Fabric Texture & Detailing] + [Colorway & Kit Elements] + [Studio Lighting & Environment] + [Camera & Technical Specifications] + [Quality & Negative Constraints]
```

### Optimal Aspect Ratios for E-Commerce
* **`1:1` (Square - 1024x1024):** Primary e-commerce catalog grid, cart line items, search results, and thumbnail cards.
* **`3:4` (Portrait - 896x1152):** Product Detail Page (PDP) main gallery views and mobile hero cards.
* **`16:9` (Landscape - 1344x768):** Editorial collection banners, season launch campaigns, and category headers.

### Photography & Studio Conventions
* **Presentation Format:** Premium ghost mannequin (invisible mannequin showing 3D depth and hollow inner neck collar) or structured flat-lay on clean, architectural stone/linen surfaces.
* **Lighting:** Commercial studio three-point softbox lighting. Crisp highlights defining ribbed collars, shoulder piping, and micro-mesh fabric texture. Zero harsh blown-out reflections.
* **Backdrop:** Seamless neutral off-white or light heather-gray cyclorama studio backdrop (`#F7F7F5` / `#EAEAE6`) matching the Jersey Valley Lumos surface palette.
* **Negative Prompting Constraints:** `no human head, no mannequin face, no floating limbs, no distorted stitching, no CGI plastic gloss, no vector art, no cartoon, no blur, no low-resolution compression, no copyrighted brand logos (Swoosh, Three Stripes), no watermarks`.
